# Artifact MCP v1.25.0

## Listen remembers your place on every device

- Signed-in viewers now get their saved Listen place on every browser and device. The place is
  kept in viewer-scope state (key `listen-place`) as well as in the browser, and the newer copy
  wins. Resume, Play, and a car library pick continue from it.
- The place is saved to the server at most every 30 seconds while playing, and at once when you
  pause, stop, or leave the page. Finishing a reading clears it everywhere.
- Without a connection, or where viewer state is unavailable, Listen keeps working from the
  browser's copy as before.

## Progress, chapters and errors in the Android app

- Apps that advertise the new `media.progress` capability (ADR-0022) receive an estimated
  position and duration, the chapter list (`media.chapters`), the current chapter, a `buffering`
  state while Listen loads or reconnects, and a short reason when Listen stops on a failure. They
  can send `forward` (30 seconds) and `chapter` commands.
- Older apps receive exactly the same messages as before.

## Faster and steadier playback

- **Read further ahead.** Listen now prepares at least three blocks, or about a minute of
  listening, while one plays (at most eight blocks). It never runs more than two speech requests
  at once and backs off when the speech workers are busy.
- **Reconnecting.** When a speech stream drops and Listen retries, the app and the car show
  buffering. If the retries fail, they show the reason.
- **More speeds.** 0.75×, 0.9×, 1.1×, 1.75×, 2.5× and 3× join the speed list. The car speed button
  cycles 1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 1.75× and 2×.
- **Forward 30 seconds.** A new button next to "↶ 15 seconds", the Media Session seek-forward
  action (headsets, lock screen), and the app's `forward` command skip ahead 30 seconds. They used
  to skip a whole block.

## Schema and configuration

No schema change (40). No new configuration. Listen adds one viewer-state read per page load and
about two writes a minute while listening; these count towards `INGRESS_STATE_PER_WINDOW`.

## Deployment and rollback

Viewer script change only; no server route changes. To roll back, restore the v1.24.0 binary.
Saved places already stored under `listen-place` are ignored by older versions.
